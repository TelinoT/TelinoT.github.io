Chopper Chaos: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026. Chopper Chaos is made by ileildev ("we"). Contact: elias.piersig@icloud.com.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. There is no account and no sign-in. Your progress (coins, choppers, settings) is saved on your device only and is never sent to us. We run no analytics of our own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), your approximate location from your IP address, which ads you saw and tapped, and crash and performance data. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You choose: after your first run the game asks once whether apps may track you (Apple's prompt). If you say no, you still get ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA and the UK, Google's consent form asks first, and the game's Settings has a "Privacy choices" button to change your answer.

Buying "Ad-free" in the game stops all ads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements go to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center name, as described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions or requests: elias.piersig@icloud.com.
